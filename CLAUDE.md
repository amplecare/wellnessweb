## Project Overview

This project is for a real health promotion and workplace wellbeing consultancy called **Ample Care Ltd**, based in the UK.

The primary goal is to create a premium website that showcases the company's understanding of care sector challenges, builds trust with care providers, and gives them confidence to book a wellbeing consultation.

The website should feel calm, credible, modern, and conversion-focused. Every page should be designed to make visitors think:

> "This company understands our problems and can help us improve our staff wellbeing."

The website is not just a brochure.

It is also the entry point into a guided consultation and wellbeing programme journey — helping registered managers and care directors move from "we have a burnout/turnover problem" to booking a free consultation, receiving a tailored wellbeing package, and becoming an ongoing client.

The booking experience should replace cold enquiries and generic contact forms with a clear, reassuring, guided path to action.

---

## Business Goals

The platform should help Ample Care Ltd:

- Showcase their specialism in care-sector wellbeing, not generic corporate wellness
- Build immediate trust with time-poor registered managers and care directors
- Increase free consultation bookings
- Make the £35-per-staff-member offer feel tangible and low-risk
- Organise consultation requests and enquiries in one place
- Track leads through to booked consultations
- Present services, packages, and case studies without needing a developer for every update
- Deliver a calm, professional experience that reflects the seriousness of the sector

---

## Visitor Journey

The website should guide visitors through the following journey.

### 1. Discover

The visitor lands on a beautiful, calm homepage showcasing:

- Hero section (the core problem/promise + pricing highlight)
- The Problem section (burnout, turnover, sickness absence)
- Our Solution / how it works (assessment → insight → plan → ongoing support)
- Why care providers choose Ample Care
- Testimonials (placeholder until real ones exist)
- Call-to-action throughout

The objective is to make the visitor feel understood within seconds.

---

### 2. Explore

The visitor can browse:

- About Us
- Services
- Pricing
- Insights / Blog (optional, for SEO and ongoing credibility)
- FAQs
- Contact

---

### 3. Request a Consultation

When the visitor is ready, they click **Book Your Free Wellbeing Consultation**.

They complete a short, guided form providing:

- Organisation name and type (care home, domiciliary, supported living, etc.)
- Staff/team size
- Role of the person enquiring
- Current challenges (burnout, turnover, absence, engagement — checkboxes)
- Preferred contact method and availability
- Any additional notes

The experience should be short, reassuring, and free of jargon — nothing that feels like filling out a compliance form.

---

### 4. Receive a Tailored Response

The consultation request is saved in the database.

The Ample Care team reviews the request and prepares a tailored response ahead of the consultation call, which may include:

- A short acknowledgement email confirming the booking
- An indicative package range based on staff count (Assessment Only / Assessment + Plan / Full Ongoing Support)
- Next steps and what to expect on the call

---

### 5. Confirm Engagement

After the consultation call, if the provider proceeds:

- Ample Care sends a formal proposal/quotation based on workforce size and needs
- Payment/engagement terms are shared
- Booking status is tracked internally (Enquiry → Consultation Booked → Proposal Sent → Confirmed Client)

## Services Showcase

One of the most important sections of the website.

Present the full service range clearly, each with its own detail page for SEO and credibility:

- Staff Wellbeing Assessments
- Care Worker Burnout Reviews
- Workplace Health Promotion Programmes
- Mental Health Awareness Sessions
- Stress Management Support
- Employee Engagement Improvement Plans
- Healthy Lifestyle Education
- Care Team Resilience Programmes

Each service should display:

- Clear icon/visual (purple/green, consistent style)
- Name
- Short description of what it involves
- Who it's best suited for (e.g. "Ideal for teams experiencing high sickness absence")

This section should make visitors feel confident that Ample Care has a structured, practical offering — not just vague wellbeing advice.

---

## Pricing / Packages

Instead of hardcoding pricing into the website, packages should be manageable from an Admin area if the platform grows to need one. Initially present as illustrative tiers:

- Assessment Only
- Assessment + Improvement Plan
- Full Ongoing Support

Each package should have:

- Short description
- What's included (assessment, report, plan, ongoing check-ins)
- Indicative "from £X per staff member" pricing
- "Get a Tailored Quote" button leading to the consultation form

Future packages or pricing changes should be manageable without changing code, once the platform is beyond an MVP marketing site.

---

## Admin Area (future phase)

If/when the platform grows beyond a marketing site, the admin area should allow the Ample Care team to manage the following without requiring a developer:

- Hero banners and homepage content
- Services list and descriptions
- Pricing tiers/packages
- Testimonials
- Case studies / insights content
- Consultation enquiries and their status (New → Contacted → Proposal Sent → Confirmed)
- Basic contact/CRM view of leads

Content displayed on the website should ultimately come from the database rather than being hardcoded, so the business can keep growing content (new services, new case studies, new testimonials) without major redevelopment.

---

## Brand & Design Notes

- Colour palette: purple (primary), green (secondary accent), white (dominant background) — calm and premium, not neon or "corporate tech."
- Photography: authentic UK care settings, real care workers — never generic/American stock imagery.
- Tone: practical, specialist, credible — never exaggerated claims or invented credentials/statistics.
- Mobile-first: primary audience (registered managers) will often browse on a phone between shifts.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA minimum.
